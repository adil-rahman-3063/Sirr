### 3.3 Qibla Compass Sensor Calibration
The Qibla compass relies on your hardware device's built-in magnetometer and accelerometer. Magnetic interference from nearby metal structures, electronic equipment, or uncalibrated device sensors may impact directional readings. Users should periodically calibrate their device compass by moving it in a figure-eight motion.
