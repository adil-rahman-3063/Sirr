2. Data Collection & Operating System Permissions

To provide accurate prayer schedules and Qibla navigation, Sirr interacts with specific hardware and platform capabilities:

2.1 Geographical Location Data

  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose:
    1. Astronomical Prayer Times: Daily prayer schedules (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) are calculated based on the Sun's precise astronomical angle relative to your exact latitude and longitude.
    2. Qibla Compass Direction: Computing the exact bearing pointing toward the Kaaba in Mecca, Saudi Arabia (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E).
  • Processing: On mobile devices, location coordinates are processed ephemerally on-device and are never stored on private servers. For web push notifications, rounded coordinates (~1.1 km accuracy) are used to schedule regional alerts and deleted upon unsubscription.

2.2 Magnetometer & Compass Sensors

  • Purpose: Real-time device orientation and magnetic heading to point the compass arrow towards the Qibla.
  • Processing: Sensor data is processed entirely in memory in real time. It is never logged or transmitted.

2.3 Exact Alarms & Background Scheduling

  • Permissions Requested: SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK
  • Purpose: In strict compliance with Google Play's Alarms and Reminders policy, exact alarms are used exclusively to deliver punctual prayer notifications at the exact calculated minute of each prayer, even when the phone is idle or in battery-saver mode.

2.4 Notifications

  • Permissions Requested: POST_NOTIFICATIONS
  • Purpose: Alerting the user when a prayer time arrives. Users can toggle individual notifications on/off at any time.

4. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • GDPR (European Union): Users have the right to access, rectify, or delete their local data at any time. Because Sirr does not store personal identities, clearing your app data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local storage.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): Sirr does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information with data brokers or marketing networks.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Sirr is a general spiritual companion utility that does not collect data from children under 13 years of age.

5. Technical Security & Encryption

  • TLS 1.3 Transport Security: All network communication is strictly encrypted over HTTPS.
  • End-to-End VAPID Push Encryption: Web push notification payloads are encrypted using AES-128-GCM and ECDH P-256 keys.
  • OS App Sandboxing: All local settings and prayer tracking records are isolated inside the host operating system's application sandbox.
